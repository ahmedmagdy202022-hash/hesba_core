# Report Logic

Reports are read-only.

## Stock

Current stock is calculated from opening stock and stock movements by item and location.

## Cashbox

Cashbox balance is calculated from opening balance and cashbox movements.

## Customer

Customer balance is calculated from opening balance, sales invoices, receipts, and sales returns.

## Supplier

Supplier balance is calculated from opening balance, purchase invoices, payments, and purchase returns.

## Profit

Profit uses sales line values and cost recorded at sale time.

## Security

Cost and profit data must not be returned to users without permission.
