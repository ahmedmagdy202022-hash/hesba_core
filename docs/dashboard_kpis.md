# Dashboard KPIs

Dashboard cards must read from report logic only.

## Owner

- sales today
- purchases today
- profit today
- cashbox balance
- customer dues
- supplier dues
- low stock count
- usage status

## Manager

- sales today
- purchases today
- customer dues
- supplier dues
- low stock count

Profit is hidden by default.

## Cashier

- my sales today
- my invoice count
- quick sale button
- quick customer button

No cost or profit.

## Stock Keeper

- low stock count
- out of stock count
- purchases today
- item shortcut

## Accountant

- cashbox balance
- customer dues
- supplier dues
- receipts today
- supplier payments today

Profit is optional by owner permission.
